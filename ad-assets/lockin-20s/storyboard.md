## Edit notes

- Keep type overlays out of shots 01–09. Only the final frame carries `LOCK IN`.
- Preserve the center 60% of each vertical frame as the protected social safe area. Do not crop the PDF toolbar, highlighted line, question prompt, Review Bank total, XP ring, or final lockup.
- `09_product_overview.png` is intentionally a tighter/wider source crop. It gives motion design extra room for a lateral pan before returning to vertical-safe framing.
- The final `LOCK IN` lockup is a temporary, non-persistent capture overlay over the real Dashboard hero; no product source files were altered.

## Music / sound direction

Use a 95–105 BPM minimalist electronic score: low warm pulse, sparse glassy ticks, and a restrained rising texture after the Focus Workspace shot. Avoid an anthemic drop; the final sound mark should feel precise, quiet, and resolved.
