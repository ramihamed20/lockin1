Return to what needs your attention.

Lockin keeps your wrong answers from sources that review supports, so you know what needs more attention. Every review starts from something you learned about yourself.

From the review bank.

Open the Review Center, then the bank or a subject session. A correct answer moves the question out of active review and makes it eligible for later recall; a mistake keeps it in review.

You got a supported question wrong, so you return to it in the subject’s bank. Once you correct it, it may appear later in the memory test when it meets the conditions.
