Active Study

Don’t stop at having read it.

Read a part. Test yourself. Know what you understood before you move on.

Start studying →

Each part builds on the last.

With Active Study, a prepared sheet becomes parts, and each part gives you a chance to test your understanding. At the end, a full test brings together what you studied.

Start from a prepared sheet.

Open the sheet, choose Active Study and a difficulty, then start when the option is available. Availability depends on the sheet’s questions being prepared for that difficulty, not just on having a PDF.

An example study session.

You read the first part and answer its test, then continue to the next part based on your result. When you come back to the same sheet, use the progress saved for the difficulty you chose.

At your pace, or with a Pomodoro timer.

On new prepared sheets you can set the pages and questions per test, or study with the Pomodoro style: a 25, 45 or 50 minute focus timer, then a test on the pages you read and a break, with results for each page.

Depending on your account.

A prepared sheet with published questions for the chosen difficulty. Customization and the Pomodoro style are available in the Focus reader only for new sheets prepared with a page-based question bank.
