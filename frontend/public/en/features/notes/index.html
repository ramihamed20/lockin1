A space to study in.

With Lockin’s writing and highlighting tools, you work with your sheet’s pages your way. Not just a file you read. A space you study in.

Pen, highlighter and text.

Open the sheet in the study workspace and choose the pen, highlighter or text. Your marks belong to your own workspace, with a save and sync status to keep an eye on.

An example study session.

You read a paragraph, highlight an important spot and write a review question beside it. You come back to the note on the same page instead of searching a separate file.
