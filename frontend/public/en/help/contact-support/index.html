How do I reach support?

Open Settings, then «Support», for the help center. To report a problem from your account, use «Suggest» in Settings, choose the problem category and send a short description.

Steps

  1. Open Settings, then Support.
  2. Look through the guides that fit the problem.
  3. To report it, open «Suggest» and write what happened and what you were trying to do, without your password or verification code.

Availability

The help center is open to everyone; the suggestions form is available after you sign in.
