Start here

  • What is Lockin?Lockin is a connected study environment that brings sheets, questions, notes and review together in one experience. It currently serves medicine and dentistry students in Libya.
  • How do I start Active Study?Open the sheet’s workspace, choose Active Study and a difficulty, then press «Start Active Study» when the option is available.
  • Can I write notes on the PDF?Yes. The sheet’s study workspace has pen, highlighter and text tools. Your notes belong to your account’s workspace.
  • How do I review my mistakes?Open the Review Center, then the review bank or «Start review» when there are active questions. Mistakes from supported sources are the ones that enter this system.
  • How do I reach support?Open Settings, then «Support», for the help center. To report a problem from your account, use «Suggest» in Settings, choose the problem category and send a short description.
