How do I set the number of pages and questions in Active Study?

On new prepared sheets, press «Customize» before you start, then set up to 10 pages per test and up to 30 questions. The final test stays the same, and you can go back to the standard style at any time.

Steps

  1. Open the sheet and choose Active Study and a difficulty.
  2. Press «Customize» if it appears, and set the pages per test and the questions.
  3. The highest number of questions depends on the pages in the shortest part, because each page provides up to 15 questions.
  4. Changing the settings of a custom session you started opens a new session after you confirm, and the earlier session’s progress is kept in the archive.

Availability

A prepared sheet with published questions for the chosen difficulty. Customization and the Pomodoro style are available in the Focus reader only for new sheets prepared with a page-based question bank.
