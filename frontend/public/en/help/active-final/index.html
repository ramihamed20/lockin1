Is there a test at the end of Active Study?

Yes. After the parts there is a final test that brings together what you studied. Its number of questions depends on the sheet’s setup, not one number for every subject.

Availability

A prepared sheet with published questions for the chosen difficulty. Customization and the Pomodoro style are available in the Focus reader only for new sheets prepared with a page-based question bank.
