How do I review my mistakes?

Open the Review Center, then the review bank or «Start review» when there are active questions. Mistakes from supported sources are the ones that enter this system.

Steps

  1. Open the Review Center.
  2. Choose «Open Review Bank» or «Start review» if it appears.
  3. Choose the subject, answer the questions shown, then check your review result.

Availability

Review collects mistakes only from questions that support review.
