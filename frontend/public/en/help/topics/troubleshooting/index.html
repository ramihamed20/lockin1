Troubleshooting

Answers that help you find the next step.

  • Why is a subject or sheet missing?Your study path may not be supported or have published content yet, or the sheet may not be openable. Check the study path on your account, and don’t change it just to try: changing it can affect your progress.
  • Why do I see a message that there are no questions?Publishing a sheet and publishing its questions are separate. A file can be available for reading before questions are available for the chosen subject or source.
  • What should I do if a sheet won’t open?Check your connection, that the sheet is available and your account’s access, then use retry if it appears. If the problem continues, describe it from Settings without sharing a protected file or sensitive account details.
  • What should I do if my notes didn’t sync?Wait for the save or sync status shown and check your connection. Don’t clear browser data or sign out as a first step before you are sure your work is saved.
  • What should I do if Active Study won’t start?Check that the sheet and difficulty are ready and your connection, and use retry if it appears. You can continue with Normal Study when it’s available; we don’t assume the sheet’s questions are ready.
  • How do I reach support?Open Settings, then «Support», for the help center. To report a problem from your account, use «Suggest» in Settings, choose the problem category and send a short description.
