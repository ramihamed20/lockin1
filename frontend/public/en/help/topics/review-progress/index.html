Review and progress

The review bank, the memory test and your recorded activity.

  • How do I review my mistakes?Open the Review Center, then the review bank or «Start review» when there are active questions. Mistakes from supported sources are the ones that enter this system.
  • Is every wrong answer saved in the review bank?Your mistakes in questions that support review are saved. Mistakes from other activity, or attempts that never reached the server, don’t enter the review bank.
  • What happens if I get the same question wrong again?The review bank keeps the question and a record of the repeated mistake, instead of treating it as a new question each time, so you can return to the point that needs attention.
  • Where does a question go after I answer it correctly in review?It leaves the active review list and becomes eligible for later recall under the review policy. Leaving the active list does not delete its record.
  • What does an empty review bank mean?There are no active questions that need review right now. The record of mastered questions may still be kept, and it doesn’t mean your data was lost.
  • What is Weekly Recall?A periodic memory test using eligible questions you have already corrected in the review bank. It appears when suitable questions are available for your account.
  • Why does Weekly Recall show as not ready?There may be no eligible mastered questions yet. Start by correcting questions in the review bank; the system won’t create an empty test just because a week has passed.
  • How do I start the memory test?From the Review Center, use the button to start or continue the memory test when it appears. If it isn’t available, work through the review bank first.
  • What happens to mistakes in the memory test?Questions you answer wrongly in Weekly Recall return to the active review bank, based on the test result.
  • How do I follow my progress?Open Progress to see your recorded activity, progress points and their history, and your streak days. These are usage indicators, not a guarantee of an exam grade or an academic comparison.
  • Where do I find my weekly summary?When your weekly summary is ready, the app shows «Your summary for the week is ready»; press «Open» to read it. A summary may not appear without enough activity.
