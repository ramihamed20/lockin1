Account and privacy

Access and the privacy of your study data.

  • Can other students see my notes?Your own notes belong to your account and the document. If you choose to share a copy of your PDF marks, students with access to that sheet can see it. Marks you don’t share stay private, and the public pages never show your notes, answers or files.
  • What appears in search and what stays private?Only the introduction, feature and guide pages are public. Study materials, account data, notes and answers stay behind the app’s access controls and are not in the public sitemap.
  • How do I protect my account data?Don’t share your password, verification codes or recovery links with anyone. Use your own account to reach the study data linked to it.
  • Does the introduction page give me access to every sheet?No. The page only explains how Lockin works. Access to a sheet or questions depends on your study path, the published content and your account or subscription access.
