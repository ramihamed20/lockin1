Studying

Sheets, notes, Active Study and questions.

  • How do I open a sheet to read it?Choose a subject, then a sheet and an available edition. Open the study workspace and use Normal Study for reading and notes.
  • Can I write notes on the PDF?Yes. The sheet’s study workspace has pen, highlighter and text tools. Your notes belong to your account’s workspace.
  • How do I write on the sheet’s pages?Open the sheet in the study workspace, choose the pen from the toolbar, then write or draw on the page.
  • How do I highlight the important parts?Use the highlighter in the study workspace to mark important places on the page. Highlights are part of your workspace notes, not an edit to the original file.
  • How do I erase what I wrote?Choose the eraser to remove marks you made in the workspace. The eraser never removes the original PDF text.
  • Can I add typed text?Yes. A text tool sits next to the drawing tools in the study workspace, for typed notes close to the page you are reviewing.
  • Does writing change the original sheet?Marks and notes are saved in the workspace linked to the sheet. Using them does not edit the content of the original published file.
  • What is Active Study?A study mode that splits the sheet into parts, with a short test after each part and a final test at the end. On new prepared sheets you can choose the pages and questions per test, or use the Pomodoro style: a focus timer, then a test on the pages you read. The standard style stays available.
  • How do I start Active Study?Open the sheet’s workspace, choose Active Study and a difficulty, then press «Start Active Study» when the option is available.
  • Is Active Study available for every sheet?No. It needs the sheet and suitable questions for the chosen difficulty to be prepared. Having a PDF alone does not make a sheet ready for Active Study.
  • Can I choose the difficulty of Active Study?Yes. Choose easy, medium or hard. On new prepared sheets you can customize up to 10 pages and 30 questions per test, or choose the Pomodoro style, which tests the pages you read once the focus timer ends with up to 40 questions, depending on what’s available. Progress is kept separately for each style.
  • What happens after I read the sheet’s pages?After reading the available pages, open the part’s test. Passing it unlocks the next pages, and earlier pages stay readable. The test is tied to the pages set for that study session.
  • Is there a test at the end of Active Study?Yes. After the parts there is a final test that brings together what you studied. Its number of questions depends on the sheet’s setup, not one number for every subject.
  • How do I return to an earlier Active Study session?Use exit with save, then open the same sheet with the same difficulty and style to resume. Custom session settings stay saved, and offline results only count once the server confirms them.
  • What happens if I restart Active Study from the beginning?After you confirm, restarting returns the session for the chosen difficulty and style to the first pages. Changing the settings of a custom session you started asks you to confirm a new session; the earlier session stays in the archive and your PDF notes are unaffected.
  • What is the difference between Normal Study and Active Study?Normal Study is for reading and working on the sheet; Active Study adds parts, questions and a final test on prepared sheets. The PDF tools stay part of the study workspace.
  • Where do I find the questions?Open Questions in your account, then the source and subject available for your study path. Published questions differ by subject and study path.
  • Does Lockin support practical dentistry questions?Some practice sources include questions based on slides and images, or on locating a spot. Their availability depends on the subject and published content; they don’t necessarily cover every subject or every student.
  • Why can’t I open Mix?The Mix source isn’t available right now. Use one of the question sources available for your study path in Questions.
  • Are there explanations for the answers?An answer explanation appears on the question and review screens when one is available. Not every question has a published explanation.
  • How do I study with the Pomodoro technique in Lockin?Choose Pomodoro style in Active Study inside the Focus reader. You read with a focus timer, test yourself on the pages you reached when it ends, then take a break. Every four tests earn a long break.
  • How do I set the number of pages and questions in Active Study?On new prepared sheets, press «Customize» before you start, then set up to 10 pages per test and up to 30 questions. The final test stays the same, and you can go back to the standard style at any time.
