Lockin Help

Getting started

What Lockin is, supported study paths and reaching your materials.

  • What is Lockin?Lockin is a connected study environment that brings sheets, questions, notes and review together in one experience. It currently serves medicine and dentistry students in Libya.
  • Who is Lockin available for?Medicine students in Tripoli, and First and Second Year dentistry students in Tripoli, Benghazi and Zawiya. The subjects and questions each student sees depend on their study path and the content published for it.
  • Does Lockin cover every university and year?Not yet. The study paths you can choose now are dentistry (First and Second Year) in Tripoli, Benghazi and Zawiya, and medicine in Tripoli (Batch 60 and 61). Published content differs between paths, and other universities and years are not supported yet.
  • Do I need an account to study?Yes. The introduction pages and the help center are public, while study materials, progress and notes live inside your account and follow its access.
  • How do I reach my sheets?Start from Materials in your account, then choose the subject and an available sheet.
  • Why are my subjects different from a classmate’s?Your subject list depends on the study path and the year or cohort registered on your account, and on the published content. A different path can mean different subjects and questions. In Second Year Dentistry, a student may also add up to two First Year subjects, which then appear for them only.
  • What is the difference between the sheet editions shown?A sheet may show a University edition and a Lockin edition when both are available. Only published editions that can be opened appear; not every sheet has both.
  • Where do I find information about Lockin inside the app?Open Settings, then «About Lockin» for the introduction and features, or «Support» for the help center and its guides.
  • Can I add First Year subjects as a Second Year dentistry student?Yes. Second Year Dentistry students can add up to two First Year subjects they are studying this year, from the «Add a First Year subject» button in Materials.
