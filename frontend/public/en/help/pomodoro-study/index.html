How do I study with the Pomodoro technique in Lockin?

Choose Pomodoro style in Active Study inside the Focus reader. You read with a focus timer, test yourself on the pages you reached when it ends, then take a break. Every four tests earn a long break.

Steps

  1. Open a new prepared sheet in the Focus reader and choose Active Study and a difficulty.
  2. Choose Pomodoro style, then the timer length: 25 minutes of focus and 5 of break, 45 and 10, or 50 and 10.
  3. Read until the timer ends, then choose the number of questions (up to 40) to test the pages you read in that session.
  4. Review your result page by page, and tap any weak page to read it again.
  5. After every four page tests you get a long break, and the final test brings together everything you studied.

Availability

A prepared sheet with published questions for the chosen difficulty. Customization and the Pomodoro style are available in the Focus reader only for new sheets prepared with a page-based question bank.
